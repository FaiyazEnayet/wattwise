Is more measurement worth buying?

Compare an intervention with doing nothing under two declared savings scenarios. The probability is your assumption, not a learned estimate. Perfect information sets an upper bound on what resolving this uncertainty could be worth.

Discounted energy-charge benefit uses the September 2026 observed energy price mix as a constant assumption. Maintenance, equipment degradation, future tariffs and comfort effects are not inferred. Do not buy equipment to chase a small timing benefit. Check the conservative payback, equipment energy, comfort, noise and lease restrictions first. Always-on usage includes essential refrigeration and other necessary loads.
